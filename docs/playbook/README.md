# Reporting and writing for LagunaBeach.md

Start with the [Student Reporting Guide](STUDENT-REPORTING-GUIDE.md).
It teaches research, preparation, interviews, and article writing through a complete example.
It assumes a capable writer who is learning reporting, with an adult editor supporting the work.

| When you need to… | Read… |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a story, prepare a visit, or interview someone | [Student Reporting Guide](STUDENT-REPORTING-GUIDE.md) |
| Copy a research worksheet or interview notes sheet | [Reporting worksheets](STUDENT-REPORTING-GUIDE.md#2-research-before-you-contact-someone) |
| Find questions for a store, gallery, farm, or sports team | [Questions by subject](STUDENT-REPORTING-GUIDE.md#5-questions-by-subject) |
| See notes become a finished draft | [Worked example](STUDENT-REPORTING-GUIDE.md#8-worked-example-from-notes-to-article) |
| Check article structure, citations, and house style | [Article Playbook](ARTICLE-PLAYBOOK.md) |
| Follow the editorial and publishing stages | [Write/Rewrite Pipeline](REWRITE-PIPELINE.md) |
| Resolve a disputed claim or audit sources | [Fact-check Pipeline](FACTCHECK-PIPELINE.md) |
| Prepare an ambient recording for the soundscape page | [Soundscape Playbook](SOUNDSCAPE-PLAYBOOK.md) |

The student leads reporting and drafting. The adult handles arrangements, supervision,
editorial review, and publication. The student guide supplies the practical workflow;
the Article Playbook remains the authority for article format and house style.

The technical sections cover Markdown, metadata, and publishing checks. You do not need
to learn those before your first interview. Bring an accurate draft and its evidence to
the adult editor, who follows the publishing pipeline.
